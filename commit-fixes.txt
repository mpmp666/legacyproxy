proxy: stop idle kicks, ghosts and invisible modern players

Three separate causes, all reported from real play:

* Random kicks of idle players. The bridge only ever spoke when the 0.14 client
  did, so a player standing still looked like a dead session and the backend
  dropped it on its RakNet timeout (10s). The modern session now sends its own
  ConnectedPing every 2s.

* Ghosts. When the 0.14 client left, the proxy just closed its UDP socket, so
  the backend kept the player in the world until its own timeout — up to a
  minute, because the legacy side waited 60s before even noticing the client was
  gone. The legacy timeout is now 15s, and the backend is told properly with a
  RakNet DisconnectionNotification (sent a few times, since it is one unreliable
  datagram) from both the legacy-disconnect path and the read-loop exit.

* Modern players invisible to 0.14 clients, and chat that could be sent but not
  received. Both were packets delivered while the old client was still on its
  loading screen: it has no level yet and silently drops them. AddPlayer and
  TextPacket are now queued until the client has actually spawned (PLAYER_SPAWN,
  after the first chunks), then flushed with the entity's current position.

Also: the read loop no longer dies on a packet the resynchroniser cannot place.
The scan is now prefixed by a raw-deflate probe (a candidate at the wrong stream
offset is noise and will not inflate), which makes it fast enough to give up
gracefully, and the duplicate-region window was widened.
