legacy: the 0.14 AddPlayer packet was missing its uuid field

Both 0.14.3 references (Nukkit-0143 and MPMPESCore) encode AddPlayer as
[uuid 16][string name][long eid][x][y][z][speed*3][yaw][headYaw][pitch][slot][metadata],
but the bridge wrote it without the leading uuid (and without the empty held-item slot
before the metadata terminator).

The client therefore read the first sixteen bytes of the name as the uuid, failed to make
sense of everything after it and dropped the entity — so a 0.14 player never saw anyone,
modern or otherwise. Worse, the malformed packet left the client in a state where it no
longer processed incoming chat: chat could be sent but not received, and only when another
player was nearby (which is what triggers the AddPlayer).

Also in this change:
* the proxy now keeps the backend session alive with its own ConnectedPing, so idle players
  are not dropped on the backend's 10s RakNet timeout;
* leaving is announced with a RakNet DisconnectionNotification and the legacy-side timeout
  dropped from 60s to 15s, so the backend no longer keeps a ghost in the world;
* AddPlayer and TextPacket are queued until the 0.14 client has actually spawned
  (PLAYER_SPAWN, after the first chunks) instead of being sent while it is still loading,
  where the client silently drops them;
* the read loop survives a packet the resynchroniser cannot place: the scan is prefixed by
  a raw-deflate probe so it is fast, and it gives up instead of taking the session down.
